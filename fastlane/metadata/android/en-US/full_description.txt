PSBDx SVN is a modern, fully open-source Subversion client for Android. Check out repositories, edit files, commit and review history from your phone, with your saved credentials protected by biometrics.

<b>Subversion tools</b>
• Checkout, update, commit, revert and cleanup
• Built-in file explorer: view, edit, rename, delete, copy, cut, paste and add files and folders
• Conflict resolution: keep mine, take theirs, or merge by hand
• Commit history with author, date, message and changed paths
• Visual diff of your working copy against the base revision
• Saved repository bookmarks with custom aliases

<b>Bring files in and out</b>
• Upload files or whole folders from internal storage, SD cards and USB drives with the built-in file browser
• Export a repository to your Download folder as a clean copy without .svn metadata

<b>Your credentials stay yours</b>
• Repository URL and username copy with one tap
• Viewing or copying a password requires fingerprint, face, or your device PIN / pattern
• Copied passwords are wiped from the clipboard after 30 seconds
• Credentials are stored with Android Keystore backed encryption
• System cloud backup is disabled for the app

<b>Backup and restore, the F-Droid way</b>
• Encrypted backup (AES-256-GCM with your own passphrase) to a local file, works with no account at all
• Optional Google Drive backup using OAuth 2.0 with PKCE in your browser: no Google Play Services and no closed-source SDKs
• Automatic backup whenever you add or change a repository, plus manual Backup Now and Restore

<b>Design</b>
• Material 3 interface with light and dark themes, automatic system switching and a manual override

<b>Good to know</b>
• Supports http, https and svn:// repositories (svn+ssh is not supported)
• Browsing storage to upload files uses the "manage all files" permission, requested only when you first upload
• Google Drive backup is only available in builds that include Google client credentials
